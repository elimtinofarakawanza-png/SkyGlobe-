SkyGlobe is a global airline based in Singapore, connecting passengers to 86 destinations across Asia, Europe, Oceania, and Africa. Founded in 2001, SkyGlobe is known for comfort, efficiency, and its elegant teal–white branding.

About SkyGlobe

SkyGlobe was founded in 2001 with a bold vision: Fly Beyond the Horizon. Over 25 years, it has grown from a regional carrier into a modern global airline.

Fleet (2026)

SkyGlobe operates a modern fleet of 96 aircraft:

  • Airbus A321XLR – 38
  • Boeing 787‑9 Dreamliner – 23
  • Airbus A350‑1000 – 14
  • Airbus A330‑900neo – 12
  • De Havilland Dash 8 Q400 – 5
  • Boeing 777‑300ER – 2
  • Airbus A350‑900 – 2

This mix supports long‑haul global expansion, efficient regional operations, and premium cabin experiences on key routes such as Singapore–London, Singapore–Paris, and Singapore–Tokyo.
