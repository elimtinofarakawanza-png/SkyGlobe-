Welcome to SkyGlobe

SkyGlobe is a global airline based in Singapore, connecting passengers to 86 destinations across Asia, Europe, Oceania, and Africa. Our mission is to deliver comfort, speed, and world‑class service to every traveler, whether you're flying for business or leisure.

About SkyGlobe

Founded in 2001, SkyGlobe Airlines began as a small regional carrier serving Southeast Asia. Over the years, we expanded into a global airline with a reputation for reliability, innovation, and exceptional service. Our leadership team has guided SkyGlobe through major milestones, including fleet modernization, international expansion, and the introduction of new cabin products. Today, SkyGlobe continues to grow with a focus on sustainability, comfort, and global connectivity.

Fleet

SkyGlobe operates a modern and fuel‑efficient fleet designed for long‑haul comfort and short‑haul reliability. Our aircraft are equipped with advanced avionics, quiet engines, and upgraded cabin interiors to ensure a smooth and enjoyable journey. From the ultra‑long‑range Airbus A321XLR to the flagship Airbus A350‑1000, every aircraft in our fleet is chosen to deliver efficiency, performance, and passenger comfort.
