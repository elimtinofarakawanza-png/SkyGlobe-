Welcome to SkyGlobe

SkyGlobe is a global airline based in Singapore, connecting passengers to 86 destinations across Asia, Europe, Oceania, and Africa. Our mission is to deliver comfort, speed, and world‑class service to every traveler. We believe flying should feel inspiring — a moment where the world opens up and new horizons become reachable. SkyGlobe was built to make every journey feel smooth, safe, and unforgettable.

About SkyGlobe

SkyGlobe Airlines began in 2001 as a small regional carrier serving Southeast Asia. Over time, we expanded into a global airline known for reliability, innovation, and exceptional service. Our growth was driven by a commitment to modern aircraft, sustainable operations, and a passenger‑first philosophy. Today, SkyGlobe continues to evolve — introducing new cabin designs, expanding routes, and improving comfort to ensure every traveler feels valued.

Fleet

SkyGlobe operates a modern and fuel‑efficient fleet designed for long‑haul comfort and short‑haul reliability. Each aircraft is selected for performance, safety, and passenger experience. Our cabins feature quiet engines, advanced avionics, and upgraded interiors that reduce fatigue and enhance comfort. From the ultra‑long‑range Airbus A321XLR to the flagship Airbus A350‑1000, our fleet reflects our commitment to sustainability, innovation, and global reach.
